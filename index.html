Ask us a question
Which kit is right for me?+

Choose Data Analyst for analytics and BI, Data Scientist for statistics, machine learning and Gen AI, or ML Engineer for model building and deployment. The All-in-One Combo brings Data Science, Gen AI and ML Engineering together. Use the career selector above to explore each kit.

What is included in a kit?+

Kits combine comprehensive handbooks, practice workbooks and solution manuals. Subjects and resource counts vary by kit. Open “View kit details” to see the selected kit’s overview.

Are these digital or physical books?+

These are digital learning resources. Handbooks and solutions are PDFs, with practice workbooks in document format. The book images illustrate the materials; physical books are not shipped.

How do I purchase and get access?+

This homepage currently lets you explore and compare kits. Checkout and digital delivery are not available on this version yet. For help with an existing purchase, email learnlytics.handbook@gmail.com.

Is this a video course or a job guarantee?+

These kits are designed for self-paced study using written materials and practice questions. The guide videos explain how to choose and use a kit. Interview preparation takes practice; the kits do not guarantee a job or placement.
